# The Core Pillar: Institutional Risk Management

<time className="text-accent-color text-sm uppercase tracking-widest font-medium mb-8 block">April 28, 2026</time>

When trading XAU/USD, risk management is not just a secondary thought—it is the core pillar of survival. Unlike traditional retail trading, which often relies on arbitrary stop losses, institutional trading employs dynamic maximum drawdown parameters.

## Understanding Drawdown

Drawdown measures the peak-to-trough decline during a specific record period of an investment. For GTC Gold, minimizing this value is our algorithm's absolute priority. By mathematically calculating lot sizes relative to equity and market volatility, we ensure your capital is insulated from erratic price spikes.
