## The Psychological Burden of Manual Trading

Human traders are inherently emotional. The fear of missing out (FOMO) and the inability to accept a loss often lead to severe execution errors:

*   **Revenge Trading:** Attempting to win back losses by increasing lot sizes inappropriately.
*   **Execution Hesitation:** Failing to execute a valid, high-probability setup because of previous consecutive losses.
*   **Inconsistent Risk Profiles:** Moving stop losses mid-trade out of hope rather than logic, destroying the mathematical expectancy of the strategy.
