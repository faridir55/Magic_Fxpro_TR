## The Mathematical Barrier: Mitigating Drawdown

The greatest enemy of compound growth is not periods of low returns, but the structural damage of significant drawdowns. The mathematics of recovery are notoriously unforgiving: a 25% portfolio loss requires a 33% gain merely to break even, while a 50% loss requires a staggering 100% gain to restore the original capital base.
